WHAT IS CLASH OF BANG?

Established in 2018, Clash of BaNG is the premier destination for players seeking to master the official Clash of BaNG game. With active regional hubs in Jakarta, Bandung, and Sanur, this growing fan community brings the digital experience to life in the real world, connecting players who share the same passion.

Sharpen every skill with beginner-to-pro tutorials or explore a vast library of troop guides and hero tactics. From expert combat blueprints to stunning community art, every tool needed for victory is ready.
