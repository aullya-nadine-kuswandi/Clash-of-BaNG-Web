THE LEGACY OF BANG

Explore the chronicles of Clash of BaNG. From the first spark of creation in 2018 to the ultimate evolution of the world's premier fan community.

OUR STORY

Clash of BaNG was founded in 2018 as a small online forum for fantasy strategy game fans. It began as a place to share troop guides and battle strategies, but quickly grew into an active community, where players contributed fan art and gameplay tips. Today, the community has regional hubs in Jakarta, Bandung, and Sanur, connecting fans through their shared passion for the Clash-style fantasy universe.

The community was started by three friends who loved fantasy strategy games and competitive battles. Their fascination with Dark Troops later became the main identity of Clash of BaNG.

KEY MILESTONE

2018: GENESIS

The First Spark

Clash of BaNG was founded in 2018 as a small online forum created by three fantasy strategy game fans. It started as a place to share troop guides and battle strategies.

2020: ASCENSION

One Thousand Warriors

Clash of BaNG officially surpassed 1,000 active members. This growth solidified the platform as a premier destination for expert combat blueprints and form strategies.

2022: REGIONAL RISE

Jakarta Hub Established

The Jakarta Hub became the first official regional community center of Clash of BaNG. It helped gather face-to-face meetups for local players and fans.

2024: EXTENSION

Bandung Awakens

The legion expanded its reach with the launch of the Bandung Hub. This creative stronghold focused on integrating local player insights and vibrant community art.

2025: COASTAL ERA

Sanur Horizon

The community established the Sanur Hub, marking its expansion into coastal territories. This hub connected regional strategists through tournaments and community engagement.

2026: PRESENT DAY

The Ultimate Vanguard

Clash of BaNG stands today as a world-class fan community. The platform continues to evolve, providing master-level tutorials and elite troop guides for the entire legion.
